## 1. Classification Methodology

To ensure strict academic honesty and eliminate decorative claims, every entry in the Paper Registry is classified into its exact academic publication category:
- **Peer-Reviewed Journal Article (PR-J):** Published in recognized peer-reviewed economics, finance, or engineering journals.
- **Peer-Reviewed Conference Paper (PR-C):** Published in peer-reviewed computer science conferences (e.g., ICLR, NeurIPS, IEEE).
- **Working Paper / Preprint (WP):** SSRN, arXiv, or institutional working paper series.
- **Book / Monograph (BK):** Academic textbook or reference volume.
- **Presentation / Conference Abstract (ABS):** Industry presentation or non-peer-reviewed conference talk.
